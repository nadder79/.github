# DARA — AI-Powered Drone Ecosystem for Telecom Networks 👋

### Welcome to DARA!

We're building an **AI-driven, drone-based ecosystem for telecommunications
network services** — replacing manual site visits and drive tests with
automated aerial data capture and AI-powered analysis.

## What we do

- **RF measurements, benchmarking & optimization** — drive-test-style RF
  surveys captured by drone, analyzed and benchmarked against network KPIs.
- **O-RAN rApps** — purpose-built rApps for coverage, quality, and
  interference use cases, built on top of captured network data.
- **Digital twin towers** — 3D digital twins of tower sites from drone
  capture, for remote inspection, planning, and change tracking over time.
- **Customer portal** — a single place for telecom operators and
  sub-contractors to request and review tower inspections, line-of-sight
  surveys, thermal imaging, LiDAR scans, and RF analysis — every service,
  one platform.
- **A new revenue stream for drone operators** — existing drone operation
  companies can plug into DARA to add telecom-specific services to their
  portfolio, without building that capability from scratch.

DARA is in active, early-stage development. Repos are being added here as
they're ready to share publicly.
